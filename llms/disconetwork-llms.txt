# Disco: Commerce Media Network

> Disco is the commerce media network that connects brands to high-intent consumers through AI-powered, personalized offers across every digital touchpoint.

## Company

- Name: Disco (Disco Technology Inc.)
- Website: https://disconetwork.com
- Founded: 2019
- Headquarters: New York, NY
- Category: Commerce Media / Advertising Technology / Post-Purchase Monetization

## Key Stats

- 800+ publisher surfaces across checkout, order confirmation, email, SMS, mobile apps, and order tracking
- 160M+ shoppers reached across the Disco network
- 1,000+ DTC and enterprise brands in the network
- AI-powered matching engine processing offers in under 200ms

## Products

### DiscoOffers
Post-purchase monetization platform for publishers. Native, AI-powered offers that consumers love. On every surface from checkout to inbox. Publishers earn incremental revenue by showing personalized third-party offers to their customers.
URL: https://disconetwork.com/offers

### DiscoBeat
Headless commerce media API for SaaS platforms. One integration gives every merchant on a platform a white-labeled monetization layer. Built for platforms like Shopify apps, loyalty programs, and commerce SaaS.
URL: https://disconetwork.com/beat

### DiscoMix
AI-powered cross-sell intelligence. Turns every post-purchase moment into incremental revenue through personalized product recommendations across complementary brands.
URL: https://disconetwork.com/mix

### OffersAI
Disco's AI engine that matches the right offer to the right consumer at the right moment. Powered by cross-brand purchase data, identity graph, and real-time behavioral signals. Processes personalization in under 200ms.
URL: https://disconetwork.com/offersai

## How Disco Is Different

1. **AI-First**: OffersAI personalizes every offer in real time using cross-brand purchase data
2. **Brand-Safe Network**: Curated network of premium DTC and enterprise brands. No junk ads
3. **Full-Funnel**: Pre-purchase, post-purchase, email, SMS, mobile, order tracking
4. **Performance-Based**: CPC and CPA pricing. Advertisers only pay for results
5. **Deterministic Attribution**: Closed-loop tracking from impression to purchase
6. **Two-Sided Marketplace**: Both publishers and advertisers benefit from the same network
